Avoid materializing full-GLM prefill head scores

Fuse FP8 indexer dots and the 32-head reduction into compact score keys
for full GLM long prefill. Reuse the existing allocation for wider query
tiles and retain the exact selector, tie order, and original single-query
GEMM tiles/tails. KV allocations, decode, and Flash dispatch are unchanged.

The materialized per-head score path was introduced in 78c0bb4f6225
(M3 layer phase, 2026-08-28). Full GLM adopted it in 8464e6a7b9bd
(Serve the full GLM-5.3 on four nodes, 2026-09-13). After the partitioned
selector fix, producing and rereading this matrix still accounted for
72.1% of indexed-layer GPU time at 256K and 2048 query tokens.

The full-geometry benchmark now covers BF16/FP8/FP4 KV. At 256K, one
indexed layer improves from 1154.657 to 263.527 ms with BF16 KV and from
1163.432 to 277.254 ms with FP4 KV, without changing the scratch budget.

Validation: 49 DSA regressions; expanded bitwise score/selection checks
across 18 shapes; a focused layer test for original and newly created
tail boundaries; zero CUDA memcheck/synccheck errors; 10 full-GLM forward,
prefill, decode, multi-rank and graph CTests. Full-model comparison pending.
